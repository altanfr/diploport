DAAD Scholarships 2027 for International Students

The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) offers fully funded master's scholarships in Germany for outstanding graduates. The scholarship covers a monthly stipend of 992 euros, health insurance and travel allowance. The programme lasts between 12 and 24 months and is taught in English or German. Applicants must hold a bachelor's degree completed within the last six years and have at least two years of professional experience.

Eligibility

Applications are open to international students from all nationalities, including candidates from Turkey, who meet the academic requirements. Applicants must submit their documents through the DAAD portal.

Deadline

The application deadline is 30 November 2026.
